# Sources and background

These sources informed the problem definition and boundaries of this resource. They do not endorse Eterna Clarity.

- Canadian Centre for Cyber Security — Developing your business continuity plan (ITSAP.10.005)  
  https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/developing-your-business-continuity-plan-itsap10005
- Canadian Centre for Cyber Security — Cyber supply chain security for small and medium-sized organizations (ITSAP.00.070)  
  https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/cyber-supply-chain-security-small-medium-sized-organizations-itsap00070
- Canadian Centre for Cyber Security — Vendor diversification (ITSAP.10.006)  
  https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/vendor-diversification-itsap10006

The Cyber Centre guidance supports identifying critical operations and dependencies, maintaining awareness of third parties, and planning alternate resources or suppliers. This Eterna Clarity resource deliberately reduces that broader work to a small human-facing dependency map; it is not a substitute for a full continuity or supplier-risk program.
