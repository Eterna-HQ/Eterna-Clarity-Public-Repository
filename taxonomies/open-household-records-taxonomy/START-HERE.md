# Household Records Filing Guide

If you just want to organize household records, start here. The formal technical model behind this guide is the **Open Household Records Taxonomy (OHRT)**.

## The simple rule

Give each record **one main home based on what it is mostly about**. Keep its other connections as notes, tags or references instead of creating competing copies everywhere.

Examples:

| Record | Main home | Other useful connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home insurance policy | Insurance | Home & property |
| Vehicle repair invoice | Vehicles & transportation | Receipts & warranties |
| Passport | Identity & civil status | Travel when relevant |
| Pet insurance policy | Insurance | Pets |
| Pay statement | Employment & income | Tax when relevant |

If a record could fit in several places, ask: **If this disappeared, which part of household life would notice first?**
