# One-Task Handoff

Use one copy for one task.

## What needs to happen?

Write the task in plain language.

> 

## When does it need to happen?

Include a date, time or trigger if there is one.

> 

## Who is taking it over?

> 

## Where are the files or information?

Name the folder, document, app, email thread or other place they should use. Do not put passwords or secrets here.

> 

## What is the next step or decision?

What should the person actually do first, or what decision are they waiting to make?

> 

## What could block it, or when should they ask for help?

Include the point where guessing would be worse than asking.

> 

## Optional contact

Who should they ask if they get stuck?

> 

## Quick handoff check

Before relying on this handoff, ask the person taking over:

1. What would you do first?
2. What information are you still missing?
3. What would make you stop and ask for help?

If their answer is not what you expected, update the handoff.
