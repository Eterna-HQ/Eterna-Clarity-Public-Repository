# Task Handoff Builder

**Version 1.0.0.** A simple way to give someone what they need to take over one task without a meeting or a long explanation.

Use it for a household job, a recurring responsibility, a project task, a client follow-up, an appointment, an order, a volunteer role, or anything else that another person may need to pick up.

## What goes in

Answer six practical questions:

1. What needs to happen?
2. When does it need to happen?
3. Who is taking it over?
4. Where are the files or information?
5. What is the next step or decision?
6. What could block it, or when should they ask for help?

That is enough for many everyday handoffs. Add more only when the task actually needs it.

## A good handoff is specific

Instead of:

> Keep an eye on the supplier order.

Write:

> Call Northside Supply by Thursday at 2 p.m. if the delivery confirmation has not arrived. The order number is in the shared Purchases folder. If the delivery moves past Friday, ask Priya before accepting the new date.

The goal is not to document everything you know. It is to give the other person enough context to act without guessing.

## Before you send it

Ask the person taking over to read the handoff and tell you:

- what they would do first;
- what they still need from you;
- what would make them stop and ask for help.

If their answer surprises you, fix the handoff before you rely on it.

## Privacy

Do not put passwords, recovery codes, private keys or other secrets in a handoff. Link to the approved place where information is stored instead of copying sensitive material into the card.
