# A short household information plan

This example is entirely fictional. It is not a completed legal document or permission to act for anyone.

Morgan will be away for two weeks. Sam needs a few practical details, not a copy of every household record.

| Part of the plan | Example entry |
| --- | --- |
| Contact first | Alex, a trusted family contact. Contact details are in the household contact list. |
| Household overview | The washing-machine repair notes and warranty are in the Home folder. |
| Things in progress | The repair shop is due to confirm an appointment by Thursday. Sam should check the message thread that afternoon. |
| Regular responsibilities | Put out the bins on Tuesday and check the pet-care calendar each morning. |
| Access and documents | Sam knows where the shared instructions are kept. No passwords or recovery codes are copied into this plan. |
| Try the plan | Sam finds the appointment details and pet-care calendar without using Morgan's phone. The repair contact was missing, so Morgan adds where to find it. |

For your own plan, keep real contact details and completed copies private. Listing a person in the plan does not give them legal permission to act.
