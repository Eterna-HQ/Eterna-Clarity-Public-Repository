# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-25

**Status:** Public review candidate

Initial candidate includes:

- a five-question handoff model: who matters, what exists, where the real information lives, what is happening now, and what needs to keep happening;
- a browser-readable editable Markdown template;
- a formatted human workbook;
- separate spaces for the household handoff map, open loops, recurring responsibilities, protected-access arrangements, governing-document pointers, and handoff testing;
- an explicit natural-authority boundary so the plan points to governing records and systems instead of becoming a duplicate authority;
- an explicit legal-authority boundary: naming someone in the plan does not itself appoint them or change their authority;
- a security boundary against passwords, recovery codes, encryption keys, banking credentials, security answers, or other secrets;
- a deliberate scope boundary from the Family Emergency Document Checklist: first-hours emergency readiness stays there; days/weeks continuity and context live here;
- event-based and periodic review guidance.

### Before 1.0.0

- confirm that the template stays small enough to maintain and does not drift into an estate-plan substitute or credential vault;
