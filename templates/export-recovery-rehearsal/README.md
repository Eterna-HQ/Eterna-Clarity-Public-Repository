# Export and Recovery Rehearsal

**Version 1.0.0.** A practical worksheet for checking what you can actually do with an export before you rely on it.

A downloaded archive, a readable copy and a working replacement are different outcomes. This resource helps you record the difference. It is useful when changing an app, preparing a backup, handing a workspace to someone else, or checking that important information remains usable outside its usual home.

## Start here

[Download the workbook](rehearsal.xlsx), or [copy the browser-readable worksheet](REHEARSAL.md). The [fictional example](EXAMPLE.md) shows what a useful failed rehearsal looks like.

Choose one real task. For example: find a packing instruction and its attached photo, then prepare the next equipment handoff without opening the old workspace. Test the information and access that task needs. Do not start by trying to certify an entire organisation's backup.

## What is included

The workbook contains **Start Here**, **Checks**, **Example**, and **Sources and Limits**. It has editable input cells, result dropdowns and simple checks for missing evidence or unexplained exclusions. It does not automatically inspect your files. The source worksheet and example are also provided as Markdown so you can use the method in your own editor.

The workbook has no macros, external data connections or automated upload. A completed file may contain sensitive context you entered. Store and share it accordingly. No signup or payment is required by Eterna.

## What counts as a useful test

Record a representative sample, what you expected, what happened, and how you checked. Check actual attachments rather than thumbnails, destination references rather than links back to the old app, and the intended person's access rather than your own logged-in session. Test a small next action after opening or importing the sample.

Use **Not tested** for an unknown. Use **Not applicable** only with a reason. A **Pass recorded** label means the worksheet has the requested evidence fields and you entered Pass. It is not independent verification. Counts are not a recovery, security or completeness score.

## Keep the originals

Test in a separate location with appropriate permission. Do not delete the source account, overwrite live records, disable a needed service or move sensitive information to an unapproved destination merely to fill in the worksheet. A small successful sample does not establish that everything can be recovered.

Write descriptions and evidence locations, not passwords, recovery codes, secret links or copies of confidential records. Use the provider's current export and recovery instructions; this resource does not replace them.
