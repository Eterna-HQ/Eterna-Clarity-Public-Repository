# Sources and limits

Observed 16 September 2026.

This checklist is an original Eterna Clarity resource. The examples below show why it can be worth testing what actually survives an export or move. They do not cover every app or guarantee that the same issue applies to your situation.

## Examples from current official help pages

- Notion, [Back up your data](https://www.notion.com/help/back-up-your-data): an export gives you copies of your content, but it does not instantly recreate the original workspace. That is why it is worth checking the things you actually need to do with the exported copy.
- Notion, [Notion Mail inbox is going away](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-mail-inbox-is-going-away-what-to-do-next): Notion's September 2026 transition notice distinguishes synced email history from some app-specific drafts, settings and other information. Use the app's current instructions for any real deadline or export step.
- Google, [Make someone else the owner of your file](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494892?hl=en): changing who owns a folder does not automatically change ownership of every file inside it. Check the files and the other person's actual access.

These companies do not endorse this checklist. Their pages keep their own terms and can change over time.

## What this checklist cannot tell you

It does not inspect your files automatically. It cannot prove that every item was exported, restore missing app features, check legal retention requirements, perform a security audit, or tell you when it is safe to delete an account.

A small successful test is useful, but it is not proof that everything is complete.

No external user-testing result, measured time saving, adoption claim or customer outcome is claimed for this resource.
