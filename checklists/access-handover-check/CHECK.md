# Access offboarding and ownership check

For each area choose **Done**, **Needs action**, **Not sure**, or **Does not apply**.

1. **Business accounts identified** - Do you know which business systems and services this person can access?
2. **Administrator ownership retained** - Does another authorized person or business-controlled account have sufficient administrator access?
3. **Individual access changed** - Have accounts, sessions, credentials or permissions that should no longer be active been disabled, revoked or changed?
4. **MFA and recovery ownership retained** - Are recovery email addresses, phone numbers and authenticator ownership no longer dependent only on the departing person?
5. **Files and information retained** - Can the business still reach the files, mail, records and other information needed for ongoing work?
6. **Devices and physical access handled** - Have company devices, keys, badges, hardware tokens and similar property been returned, transferred or revoked as appropriate?
7. **Shared access reviewed** - If shared credentials still exist, have they been changed through the approved credential-management process where necessary?
8. **Automations and integrations transferred** - Will scheduled jobs, integrations, forwarding rules or automations continue after the person's account changes?
9. **Vendor and service portals retained** - Does the business still control important third-party portals, billing relationships and support contacts?

## Proof one real task

After the changes, have another authorized person complete one real task that depends on the transferred access or information.

**Can the business still do the work without using the departing person's identity?**

If not, mark the relevant area **Needs action**.

Do not put passwords, recovery codes, API keys or other secrets into this checklist.
