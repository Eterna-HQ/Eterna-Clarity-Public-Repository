# Family Emergency Document Checklist

**Use:** mark only what applies to your household. A checked item means the information is current and an appropriate person could actually find or use it.

## Three readiness needs

- **Know where it is** — the current or authoritative record has a known location.
- **Keep useful details or a copy accessible** — a contact route, reference number, short summary, or appropriate copy can be reached quickly when that is useful.
- **Protect the original** — sensitive originals stay in an appropriate secure home rather than being collected into one portable folder.

> Do not write passwords, PINs, recovery codes, full credentials, or unnecessary account secrets on this checklist.

## Contacts & coordination

- [ ] **Primary household emergency contacts** — Keep current names and contact routes accessible; the people who may need the plan should know where it is.
- [ ] **Out-of-area contact** — Keep one current contact route accessible in case local household members are separated.
- [ ] **Meeting / reunification locations** — Record the places household members should use if home is unavailable or people are separated.
- [ ] **Trusted person who can find key information** — Someone appropriate should know the plan exists and how to find the information without relying on your phone.
- [ ] **Local emergency / non-emergency contacts** — Keep the numbers or official contact routes that are actually relevant where you live.

## Health & care

- [ ] **Current medication list** — Keep a current, readable list of medications and key instructions accessible; do not treat it as a replacement for prescriptions or medical records.
- [ ] **Allergies and critical health information** — Keep only the information another appropriate person or caregiver may need quickly, and review it when health circumstances change.
- [ ] **Medical devices, equipment or essential supplies** — Know what is needed, where it is, and any practical information a caregiver would need during an evacuation or disruption.
- [ ] **Doctor, clinic and pharmacy contacts** — Keep current contact routes accessible if these providers are important to the household.
- [ ] **Care, accessibility or support instructions** — Record the practical information another appropriate person would need to support a household member safely.
- [ ] **Health coverage / health identifier location** — Know where the current authoritative card or record is; keep only the minimum useful reference details accessible.

## Identity & legal

- [ ] **Passports and government-issued identification** — Know where the current originals are. Keep a reference or copy accessible only when useful and appropriate; a copy is not the original.
- [ ] **Birth, citizenship or immigration documents** — Know the secure location of applicable originals; avoid carrying originals unnecessarily in a general emergency folder.
- [ ] **Will, power of attorney, personal directive or similar document** — An appropriate person should know what exists and where the authoritative document is kept. Do not assume a checklist changes legal authority.
- [ ] **Custody, guardianship or consent documents** — If applicable, know the current authoritative location and keep the practical details needed for emergency care or pickup accessible.
- [ ] **Recent household-member photos** — Optional: keep recent photos accessible if they would help identify or reunite household members during an emergency.

## Insurance & financial references

- [ ] **Home / tenant / vehicle insurance** — Keep the current insurer, contact route and policy reference accessible; the full authoritative policy can stay in its normal secure location.
- [ ] **Other relevant insurance** — For life, health, travel, pet or other applicable coverage, keep enough detail to identify the current provider and policy.
- [ ] **Claims contact information** — Keep the current claims contact route accessible for coverage likely to matter during an emergency.
- [ ] **Financial institution orientation** — Know which institutions matter and where current records live. Do not put passwords, PINs, recovery codes or unnecessary account secrets in this checklist.
- [ ] **Essential payment / access arrangements** — If the household depends on a specific practical arrangement, make sure the appropriate person knows how to locate the legitimate access method without recording secrets here.

## Children, dependants & pets

- [ ] **School / daycare emergency and pickup arrangements** — If applicable, keep current school/daycare contact information and the authorized pickup plan accessible.
- [ ] **Dependant care instructions** — Keep current practical care information for children, older relatives or other dependants another appropriate person may need.
- [ ] **Support network for disability or special needs** — If applicable, identify the people, equipment, supplies and contact routes that matter when normal support is disrupted.
- [ ] **Pet / service-animal identification and care information** — Keep current identification, veterinarian contact, medications, vaccination or licence references, and other relevant care details.
- [ ] **Pet / service-animal evacuation destination** — Know where the animal can go and what transport or carrier arrangements are needed if normal accommodation is unavailable.

## Home & evacuation basics

- [ ] **Safe exits and meeting points** — Household members should know the relevant exits and meeting locations; follow local authority instructions during an actual emergency.
- [ ] **Emergency kit / grab-and-go kit location** — Everyone who may need it should know where the kit is kept.
- [ ] **Fire extinguisher and key safety equipment locations** — Know where the equipment is. This checklist does not replace training, product instructions or emergency-service guidance.
- [ ] **Water, electrical, gas / heating and other utility control locations** — Know the locations and relevant provider contact routes; do not improvise shutoff procedures during an emergency.
- [ ] **Landlord, building management or property contact** — If applicable, keep the current contact route accessible.
- [ ] **Local hazard and official-alert information** — Know which hazards are realistic where you live and which official sources provide instructions or alerts.

## Ten-minute test

- [ ] An appropriate person can find the current emergency contacts.
- [ ] They can find the medication / critical-care information that applies.
- [ ] They can identify current insurance and the right contact route.
- [ ] They know where the relevant identity or legal originals are kept.
- [ ] They can understand dependant, pet or support arrangements that apply.
- [ ] They can do this without using your phone or asking you where everything is.

If one of these fails, fix the access path rather than simply making more copies.

## Review when life changes

- [ ] **Contact changes:** Phone number, address, trusted contact, provider or emergency-contact changes.
- [ ] **Health changes:** Medication, allergy, condition, medical-device, care or accessibility needs change.
- [ ] **Household changes:** Someone joins or leaves the household; a dependant or caregiver arrangement changes.
- [ ] **School / care changes:** School, daycare, pickup authorization, custody or support arrangements change.
- [ ] **Insurance changes:** Policy renews, provider changes, coverage changes, or a claim changes the relevant contact route.
- [ ] **Identity / legal changes:** Passport or ID renews; a legal document is created, amended, revoked or moved.
- [ ] **Pet changes:** Pet medication, veterinarian, licence, microchip, boarding or evacuation arrangements change.
- [ ] **Home / move changes:** Address, building, utility, landlord, emergency-kit location or evacuation route changes.
- [ ] **After a real emergency or drill:** Something was hard to find, unclear, out of date or inaccessible when tested.

---

This is a practical preparedness tool, not legal, medical, financial, insurance, privacy, security, or emergency-response advice. Follow current instructions from relevant authorities and professionals.
