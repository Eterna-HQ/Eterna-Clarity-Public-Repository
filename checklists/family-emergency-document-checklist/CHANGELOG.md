# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-25

**Status:** Public review candidate

Initial candidate includes:

- a three-state readiness model: known location, accessible useful details/copy, protected original;
- a browser-readable printable checklist;
- a formatted human workbook;
- emergency contacts, health/care, identity/legal, insurance/financial references, dependant/pet, and home/evacuation sections;
- explicit security boundaries against collecting passwords, PINs, recovery codes, full credentials, or unnecessary sensitive originals;
- event-based review triggers rather than an arbitrary fixed filing schedule;
- a ten-minute usability test focused on whether another appropriate person can actually find and use the information.

### Before 1.0.0

- founder paired review and approval;
- confirm intended reuse license;
- validate the checklist against varied household scenarios without turning optional items into universal requirements;
- ensure accessibility guidance remains balanced with protection of sensitive originals and credentials.
