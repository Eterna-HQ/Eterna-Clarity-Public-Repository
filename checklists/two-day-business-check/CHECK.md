# Two-day business check

For each area choose **Could continue**, **Would slow down**, **Would stop**, **Not sure**, or **Does not apply**.

1. **Customer work** - Could someone see what is promised, what is in progress and what has to happen next?
2. **Money in and money out** - Could the business issue invoices, receive payments, pay urgent bills and understand what is due?
3. **Systems and admin access** - Could an authorized backup person access the business accounts the company depends on?
4. **Critical files and information** - Could another person find the current agreements, quotes, job files, procedures or records needed to keep work moving?
5. **Suppliers and outside contacts** - Could someone reach the suppliers, contractors or advisers needed to solve an urgent problem?
6. **Urgent obligations and deadlines** - Could someone see payroll, filing, delivery, renewal or other time-sensitive obligations due in the next few days?
7. **Decisions and authority** - Would the team know what they can decide, what must wait and who to call when a decision is outside their authority?
8. **Backup and recovery** - If a critical file or system failed, does someone know what is backed up, who restores it and whether restoration has been tested?

## What to do with the result

1. Put **Would stop** items first.
2. Put **Would slow down** items second.
3. Resolve **Not sure** items by testing the handover instead of assuming it works.
4. Give each gap one concrete next action.

## Prove one Could continue answer

Before you finish, pick one area you marked **Could continue** and test it for real. Have the backup person open or use one current file, system or instruction without help from the key person. If they cannot do it, change the answer and fix the handover before relying on it.
