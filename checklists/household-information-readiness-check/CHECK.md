# Household information check

For each question choose **Ready**, **Needs work**, **Not sure**, or **Does not apply**.

1. **Emergency and health information** - Could someone find current emergency contacts, medications, allergies and essential care information without asking you?
2. **Insurance and home information** - Could someone quickly find your insurer contact, policy information and the basic records needed to describe the home and belongings?
3. **Identity and important documents** - Does someone you trust know where current identity, legal and other important documents are kept?
4. **Bills and recurring services** - Could another person tell what must be paid or kept running over the next few weeks?
5. **People, pets and care responsibilities** - Could someone take over urgent care, school, pet, appointment or household responsibilities with the information available now?
6. **Backup access to the essentials** - If your main phone, computer or home were unavailable, would a trusted person still know where to find the critical information?

## What to fix first

For every **Needs work** or **Not sure** answer, write one next step. Keep the real information in its proper secure location; this checklist should point to it rather than becoming a copy of sensitive records.

## Prove one Ready answer

Before you finish, pick one answer you marked **Ready** and test it for real. Ask the trusted person who may need the information to find one actual item without coaching from you. If they cannot find it quickly, change that answer to **Needs work** and fix the path to the information.
